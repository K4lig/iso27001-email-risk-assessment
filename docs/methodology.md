Risk Assessment Methodology
1. Scope
The scope of this risk assessment covers the enterprise email infrastructure, information transfer workflows, user authentication mechanisms, and staff awareness processes regarding the protection of personal data (PII) and institutional assets.
2. Risk Calculation Criteria
The risk level is defined using the formula: Risk = Likelihood × Impact.
Likelihood Scale (1-5)
1 - Rare: Highly unlikely to occur.
2 - Unlikely: Not expected but possible.
3 - Possible: Might occur occasionally.
4 - Likely: Expected to occur frequently.
5 - Almost Certain: Will undoubtedly happen.
Impact Scale (1-5)
1 - Negligible: Minimal operational disruption.
2 - Minor: Minor financial loss or data exposure.
3 - Moderate: Significant disruption, regulatory fines possible.
4 - Major: Critical system downtime, large-scale data breach.
5 - Severe: Catastrophic business failure, massive PII leak.
3. Risk Matrix (Inherent vs. Residual)
The resulting scores are categorized as follows to prioritize remediation efforts:
1 to 5: Low Risk (Acceptable, requires periodic monitoring)
6 to 11: Medium Risk (Requires planned mitigation)
12 to 19: High Risk (Requires urgent action and control implementation)
20 to 25: Critical Risk (Immediate escalation, containment, and blocking required)
